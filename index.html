Support

Barline — master it bar by bar. Scan a page of sheet music, pick your instrument, loop the hard bars and let the tempo ramp do what a teacher does: add 5 BPM only when the pass was clean.

Need help? Write to vdnhan97@gmail.com and include the visitor id from the App's Account screen if your question is about credits, a purchase or a song. We answer within two working days.

Common questions

  • A page was not read correctly. Open the song's Review screen: the bars we were unsure about are listed with a “Hear bar” button, and you can fix notes there. A page we could not read at all is refunded automatically.
  • Do I need a subscription to practise? No. Practice, looping, the tempo ramp, the metronome, listening and MusicXML import are free. Credits are only used when Barline reads a photo or a PDF page.
  • I bought Plus or a pack and it is not showing. Tap “Restore purchases” on the upgrade screen. If it still does not appear, send us the visitor id and the App Store receipt.
  • How do I cancel Plus? In your Apple Account settings under Subscriptions. Deleting the App does not cancel a subscription.
  • Does listening record me? No. Pitch and timing are detected on the device and audio never leaves your phone. See the Privacy Policy.
